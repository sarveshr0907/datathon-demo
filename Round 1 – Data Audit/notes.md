# Round 1 – Data Audit

## Objective

To understand the air-quality dataset and identify important data-quality issues before analysis.

## Data Understanding

- Cities: Chennai, Delhi, Mumbai
- Stations: 9 total (3 per city)
- Time period: 2021–2023
- Frequency: Hourly
- Records: 236,520 total
- Variables: 25 per dataset

## Key Variables

- Ozone – target variable
- AT – Temperature (°C)
- RH – Relative Humidity (%)
- SR – Solar Radiation (W/m²)
- Other air pollutants and meteorological variables

## Initial Data Characteristics

- Data contains hourly air-quality and meteorological observations.
- Multiple monitoring stations are available for each city.
- Missing values are present in several variables.
- Data covers three complete calendar years.

## Key Observations

1. The dataset covers three major cities across 2021–2023.
2. Ozone can be analysed against temperature, humidity and solar radiation.
3. Missing values and data-quality issues need to be assessed before statistical analysis.
