# Round 2 – Statistical Investigation

## Research Question

How are temperature, relative humidity and solar radiation associated with ground-level ozone concentration across Chennai, Delhi and Mumbai?

## Objective

To statistically investigate the relationship between ozone concentration and important meteorological variables.

## Hypothesis

- Temperature is positively associated with ozone concentration.
- Solar radiation is positively associated with ozone concentration.
- Relative humidity is negatively associated with ozone concentration.

## Variables

### Dependent Variable
- Ozone (µg/m³)

### Independent Variables
- AT – Temperature (°C)
- RH – Relative Humidity (%)
- SR – Solar Radiation (W/m²)

## Statistical Method

Correlation analysis is used to measure the strength and direction of the relationship between ozone and the meteorological variables.

## Expected Outcome

The analysis will identify which meteorological variable has the strongest association with ozone concentration and whether the relationship is positive or negative.
